Mode 01

Full Mode — the whole article, nothing else.

One click and everything vanishes: sidebars, ads, cookie banners, navigation. What remains is the text, typeset cleanly at whatever size and width suits you.

Font size and line height persist across sessions via Chrome's synced storage — your environment is always exactly how you left it.

In the beginning, there was space to think. The newspapers of the early nineteenth century carried almost no advertising — dense with text, subsidized by political parties or sold at prices only the wealthy could afford.
